Vietnam Mega-Sale Simulator

A front-end simulation of an 11.11 flash sale. The campaign status is derived from the configured schedule and the current time, and the countdown is recomputed from the wall clock on every update, so it never drifts. While the sale is live you can reserve units: they are locked for you for a few minutes, tracked by a reservation state machine. Checkout comes in a later milestone.
