Mega-Sale Simulator Tests

T-01 covers the centralized campaign configuration and its validation, status derived from the schedule and the clock, the product card, the status-driven action button, the configuration error view and mounting. T-02 covers the zero-drift countdown: wall-clock remaining time, HH:MM:SS.mmm formatting, delayed callbacks, status changes at the target and timer cleanup. T-03 covers the reservation state machine, inventory accounting, the authoritative reservation store and the reserve / cancel UI. T-04 covers expiring locks: absolute expiry, the expiry timer, idempotent release, stale callbacks, cancellation and the lock countdown. T-05 covers checkout: eligibility, deduplicated submissions, purchase, failed payments, expiry during checkout and the confirmation. T-06 covers whole-simulator integration scenarios; T-07 adds random-sequence invariant checks and the PRD 7 race scenarios. T-08 runs the whole sale workflow end to end, on a fake clock and on real timers.
